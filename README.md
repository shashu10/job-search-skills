# Job Search Workflows

Find and apply to jobs, request referrals, practice interviews and negotiate offers with your AI agent.

**Good inputs matter:** your work history, trusted feedback, personal contacts and study material make these skills more useful.

## Install

Paste this into an agent that supports installing skills from GitHub:

```text
Install all the job-search skills from https://github.com/shashu10/job-search-skills
```

Then ask:

> Set up my job search. Start with my resume, ask what roles and locations I want,
> and keep my records in a private folder. Handle the setup for me.

Already have a job-search folder? Ask the agent to use it. To try the workflow first:

> Show me how this works with a fictional candidate.

## Find and Apply to Jobs

**Try asking**

> Find jobs worth applying for, and apply after my confirmation.

**What you get:** A shortlist with fit, gaps and eligibility checks. You choose which jobs to apply to. After confirmation, the agent selects or tailors your resume, uses browser/computer-use tools to fill forms, uploads materials, submits and records the receipt or blocker.

## Find and Request Referrals

**Try asking**

> Find referrals for the jobs I've applied to. Start with people I know, draft the requests, and send after my confirmation.

**What you get:** Relevant contacts and messages to review, starting with personal contacts and existing connections before public hiring contacts. After confirmation, the agent sends the selected requests and records delivery, replies and next steps. Existing conversations and paused roles stay respected; a request isn't a completed referral.

## Ask Interview Coach

> Coach me on system design. Ask one question and let me answer before hints.

Review code, strengthen project stories, learn a concept or run a mock. Use your study material and preparation budget. Track independent work, feedback and retries. **No interview booking required.**

## Ask Offer Negotiator

> A recruiter asked my salary expectations. Help me answer and rehearse their pushback.

Discuss compensation before an offer, compare potential packages or negotiate actual terms. Separate cash from uncertain equity. Weigh the work, team, location and costs alongside pay. **The decision stays yours.**

## Keep the context

Ask **“What needs my attention?”** for the next useful action. With access to your messages, the agent can reconcile recruiter replies. Request recurring checks when you want them.

Your experience, preferences, applications and conversations stay in a private folder outside this repo. Your chosen AI provider still processes the information you share. You decide what the agent can apply to or send.

Browser, email and scheduling support depend on your agent. The optional dashboard is still planned.
