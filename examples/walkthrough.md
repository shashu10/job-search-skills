# Four everyday requests, one fictional search

Every name, role, date and event here is fictional. This is a worked example, not a live candidate record or an outcome claim. Use a disposable workspace with external sending, submission and scheduling disabled. The prompts demonstrate behavior; they are not magic keywords.

## Find jobs worth applying for

> Find jobs worth applying for from these supplied snapshots. Give me a shortlist to review.

Candidate A has confirmed product UI experience. Example Systems advertises `EX-042`, Product Engineer. A job board duplicates the employer listing. The role fits their work, but work-authorization support is unconfirmed. A second opening primarily involves infrastructure, outside the candidate's preferences.

The agent deduplicates the sources and presents fit, gaps and a proposed route. The candidate says, “Pursue EX-042 if eligibility is confirmed; pass the infrastructure role.” Those are dated role decisions. Neither is evidence of an application. An existing application grant may authorize later execution; a new statement of interest does not create one.

### Resume work follows the approved application

For the next part, supply a fictional employer clarification confirming support, clear any referral hold and explicitly authorize EX-042.

> Apply to EX-042 using my current corrected experience.

The application workflow selects the existing resume. If it needs substantive correction or tailoring, it calls the resume workflow automatically. Candidate A's older document claims ownership of an entire platform and a 40% revenue increase. Confirmed evidence supports only interaction design and frontend implementation, with no revenue metric. The new material uses the supported contribution and preserves the previously submitted version.

With only local drafting tools, the result stays prepared and reports the rendering or portal blocker. A supplied fictional receipt can establish a simulated submission; without one, the example cannot claim success. Actual submitted materials remain linked to the exact role record.

## Find referrals for my applications

> Find referral routes for the roles I've applied to. Start with people I know, then existing connections and public hiring contacts. Draft the asks for review.

Supply two submitted roles, a duplicate historical row and a paused role. A supplied contacts export identifies a former teammate at Example Systems; another person is only a network connection. An existing recruiter already handles the second role.

The agent builds a deduplicated cohort, respects the pause and existing conversation, and compares the available routes. A personal relationship has evidence; a connection label alone does not establish one. For an already-applied role, check whether the employer can associate a referral with that application. If it cannot, suggest an appropriate introduction or follow-up without resubmission. Return source coverage, recommended contacts and drafts, not claims that inaccessible networks were searched or messages sent.

## Ask Interview Coach

> I have no interviews booked. Help me practice system design for 20 minutes. Ask one question and let me answer before hints.

The coach starts from that goal and time budget. It does not require an employer, invitation or workspace. After the candidate's attempt, feedback identifies a specific gap, followed by a fresh retry. Record assistance honestly; receiving the key insight from AI does not demonstrate independent mastery.

When an actual invitation arrives, the same coach can use the recruiter instructions, exact submitted resume and available preparation time. Employer rules govern assessed work. A debrief distinguishes the candidate's observations from employer feedback and guides the next practice session.

## Ask Offer Negotiator

> A recruiter asked my salary expectations. There is no offer yet. Help me answer and rehearse their pushback.

The negotiator uses stated priorities, the employer's range if supplied and real alternatives. Private walk-away constraints stay out of outward-facing drafts. It can role-play the discussion without inventing competing offers.

For potential packages, compare conditional cash and costs over the requested horizon; keep private equity scenarios separate. Estimates never become recorded offers. When actual terms arrive, compare their version, conditions and deadline, then prepare negotiation points. The candidate makes the decision; a draft is not permission to send or accept.

## What happens between requests

Supporting inbox reconciliation updates exact-role evidence from supplied messages or authorized sources. Pipeline review answers “What needs my attention?” Scheduling is available only when requested and supported by the host. The same private facts, materials and role history connect all four entry points.
