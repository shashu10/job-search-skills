# One fictional search, connected across stages

Every name, role, date and event in this walkthrough is fictional. This is a worked example, not a live candidate record or an outcome claim. Run it in a disposable workspace with sending, submission and scheduling tools disabled.

## Experience and resume

Candidate A built a workflow editor. Their confirmed contribution covers the interaction design and frontend implementation. A peer says the collaboration was strong but supplies no measured revenue impact. An older resume claims ownership of the whole system and a 40% revenue increase without supporting evidence.

Ask `job-resume` to select or revise the material. A useful result narrows ownership to the confirmed contribution, leaves the unsupported metric out, and records which peer suggestions were accepted. It preserves any previously submitted version. A new PDF is not ready merely because rendering succeeded: its pages need review.

## Discovery and route

Example Systems advertises role `EX-042`, Product Engineer. A job board and the employer page refer to the same requisition. The employer page describes product work; work-authorization support is unconfirmed. A contact has offered to check the referral route.

Ask `job-research` for the actual fit, gaps and next action. It should retain the official source and check date, deduplicate the listing, and expose the eligibility unknown. Ask `recruiting-outreach` to draft a concise enquiry using only the confirmed contribution. A draft and an offer to refer establish neither a sent message nor a completed referral.

## Application

For the next part of the example, supply a fictional user update confirming the eligibility answer, clearing the referral hold and authorizing this exact application. Ask `job-application` to prepare it with the corrected resume.

Without a real browser/portal, stop at prepared. If the exercise later supplies a clearly fictional portal receipt, the synthetic record may advance to submitted, retaining the exact material and event date. A loading error after clicking Submit requires checking the result before retrying.

## Interview and learning

Supply a fictional recruiter message inviting Candidate A to a 45-minute project discussion on 2030-06-18 at 14:00 UTC. Preparation time is 90 minutes. External AI is allowed for practice but prohibited during the actual round.

Ask `job-interview` to build the plan from the role and submitted material. Practice explaining personal ownership, one architectural decision and one failed approach. Track which explanations were independent and which needed hints.

After a simulated practice session, record: “I could explain the UI decisions but needed help explaining persistence failures.” The next task should address that specific gap. This is self-assessment, not employer feedback or evidence of advancement. A real interview completion and next-round invitation require their own confirmation.

## Inbox, review and optional offer

`job-inbox` can reconcile supplied messages without a connected mailbox. It records the source coverage honestly and does not claim a complete inbox check. `job-pipeline-review` prioritizes the upcoming preparation and outstanding eligibility or routing decisions.

When a fictional written offer is supplied, `job-offer` compares its confirmed cash, dates, conditions and work scope, keeping uncertain equity separate. It does not infer acceptance. `job-search-schedule` can prepare a recurring review specification when asked; without a scheduler it reports that specification as uncreated.

The same facts and role history support each step. No stage needs a second live application tracker.
