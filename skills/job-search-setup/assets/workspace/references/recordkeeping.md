# Recordkeeping and evidence

Use the existing records mapped in `roles/INDEX.md`. One live application record per lane and one live outreach record are enough. Store links to relevant evidence; do not duplicate whole inboxes, sessions or biographies in every packet. Interview and offer indexes locate supporting material; inbox reviews record search coverage; schedule records locate requested jobs. Employer stage changes still have one owner. A UI reads these sources and should expose their dates and unresolved evidence rather than maintaining an independent status table.

## Identity and history

Identify a role by employer, requisition ID, employing location and application route. If no requisition exists, record the best available identity and uncertainty. Multiple countries or aliases may point to one requisition; check for duplicates across mapped lanes without taking ownership of their work.

Record submission evidence, exact submitted material/version, current stage, event date, date reported/recorded, and next action/blocker. Dates of research, file creation, reporting and submission are different. For scheduled interviews, retain timezone and confirmed versus proposed status. Never infer timezone from physical residence.

Preserve an append-only event history under each role when the stage changes. Keep original submission evidence after rejection or progression. A corrected resume is for new use; preserve the file actually sent.

For sourced roles, record the candidate's unreviewed/pursue/pass/hold decision separately from your recommendation and employer stage. Retain its date, reason, proposed application/referral route and any applicable grant link. Interest does not authorize submission or messaging. Move the role's current row as it progresses, preserving its decision history; a shortlist is part of the owning tracker, not another ledger.

## Stages

| State | Minimum evidence / interpretation |
|---|---|
| Researched / prepared | Work exists; no submission established |
| Sent, unacknowledged | Application email sent; receipt by employer not confirmed |
| Submitted / acknowledged | Portal confirmation, employer receipt or explicit user-confirmed submission |
| Interview invited | Employer selected the candidate; note stage and whether scheduled |
| Interview completed | Completion confirmed; does not itself mean passed |
| Advanced | Employer confirms the next evaluation stage |
| Offer received | Employer offer evidenced; distinguish verbal, written and conditional |
| Offer accepted | Candidate's actual acceptance confirmed |
| Rejected / not proceeding | Employer decision evidenced; reason unknown unless supplied |
| User pass / hold / closed role | Distinct from rejection and withdrawal |
| Withdrawn | Actual authorized withdrawal confirmed |

Keep self-scheduled application-entry screens separate from employer selection. Keep recruiter-led processes with unverified formal submissions visible in a separate section; do not silently add them to application totals. Outreach states are researched, drafted, sent, replied, delivery failed and closed; an introduction is not a formal referral or an application without evidence.

Immediately after an application or recruiting message, record the result in its live record before reporting completion. Include user-reported actions with their stated provenance. Preserve the action's evidence if recording fails and report the gap; do not repeat a send or submission to repair the record.

For outreach, retain relationship type, channel, origin, request intent and introduction chain when known. Keep replies, helpful offers, internal handoffs, completed referrals and interviews separate. Downstream responses are not extra independent acquisitions, and helpful unused offers are not rejections.

## Authority and corrections

Keep exact role/batch and contact authorizations with their records: user's instruction, date, scope, disclosed exceptions and later pauses. Apply current confirmed corrections within scope. Do not transfer approval between employers, roles, locations or actions. Preserve superseded evidence as history.

## Reviews and maintenance

Reconcile mutually exclusive current-status counts before reporting totals. Analyze cohorts by actual submission date where known, label unknown/report-only dates, and state an as-of date. Unresolved recent applications are not failures. Distinguish role-level and company-level counts and avoid adding company totals across lanes without deduplication. Report sample sizes and source-attribution uncertainty.

Keep research and review snapshots dated. Resume inventories and indexes are navigation, not duplicate live stage tables. Create a separate packet only for unique custom answers, a meaningful exception, a blocker or details not recoverable from shared records. Retain receipts and unique feedback; put regenerable previews in a cache. Do not save passwords, verification codes or session tokens. Limit copied mail and assessment content to what the authorized task needs.
