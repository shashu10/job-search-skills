# Confirmed candidate facts

Not yet populated. Use only candidate-confirmed facts or identified source evidence. Unknown fields may remain unknown until needed. Read corrections before reusing an older resume or form answer.

## Professional background

| Fact | Confirmed value and precision | Source | Confirmed on | Scope / supersession |
|---|---|---|---|---|

Record employment dates and titles, education, actual project responsibilities, supported skills and outcomes. Link detailed project stories or source evidence when needed; avoid copying a whole career history into every packet. Keep candidate-confirmed facts, externally documented facts, feedback and inference distinguishable. Keep total career duration distinct from specialization tenure. Separate employment, unpaid projects, educational exercises and personal experiments. Preserve historical facts even when a geography is excluded from future searches.

## Application facts

| Field | Confirmed answer | Source / date | Limits |
|---|---|---|---|

Add contact fields, work authorization by location, languages, availability and compensation answers only when relevant. Contact address, physical location, legal residence and work rights are distinct. Do not infer one from another or from the device timezone. Keep private contact details off public resumes unless the candidate chooses to share them.
