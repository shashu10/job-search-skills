# Broad company and vacancy discovery

Use this mode for a broad market scan or an explicit company list. An exact-role check or short targeted search does not require all source families.

## Search coverage

Choose complementary sources for the requested market and record what was actually covered:

| Source family | What it contributes | What still needs verification |
|---|---|---|
| Employer careers sites | Current official openings and application routes | Duties, employing location, eligibility and availability |
| General/professional job platforms | Searchable role and hiring-post leads | Duplicate, expired or syndicated listings |
| Regional or specialist boards | Employers and titles missed by general platforms | Employer identity and actual employment terms |
| Company directories and investor portfolios | A broader set of companies and product domains | Whether relevant teams or openings exist in the market |
| Hiring posts, communities and referrals | Team context and named hiring routes | Current affiliation, scope and any application/referral holds |

Search several relevant titles and duty phrases derived from the candidate's preferences. Broaden titles without relaxing hard constraints. Record query terms, location filters, check dates and material access/page limits. Distinguish a source that was searched with no useful result from a blocked or unsearched source. Extend coverage when new sources yield useful employers; do not call a small familiar-employer list exhaustive.

Use available authorized tools and sessions. Read-only discovery does not authorize creating accounts, purchasing access, requesting recruiter representation, enabling alerts or activating an autonomous application service. Coordinate shared browser work according to the workspace's resource and locking rules; delegates can consolidate local findings without navigating a shared session.

## Deduplicate and hand off

Normalize company aliases only when supported. Deduplicate vacancies by employer and exact requisition/official URL; preserve each discovery source as provenance. Anonymous agency cards remain unidentified leads until the employer and route are established, and do not count as new company coverage.

Keep two outputs when breadth is requested: a company inventory and verified role matches. Label each company as having a verified fit, only an unresolved vacancy, or no suitable opening found in the searched coverage. Do not turn an unverified lead into a pending application. Link the canonical tracker for existing applications rather than creating another current-state queue.

Rank eligible roles with explicit fit and feasibility evidence. State unresolved language, location, work-authorization or mandatory-skill questions; title similarity and an English posting are insufficient. Route an already submitted role to its current follow-up instead of recommending another application.
