# Counting, cohorts and attribution

Read for a full breakdown or any application, interview, outreach or referral rate. Establish source coverage and cutoff first. Exact evidence takes precedence over a stale headline; preserve an unresolved discrepancy instead of forcing totals.

## Applications and stages

Count each evidenced role/application route once according to the workspace's documented convention, using requisition identity where available. Repeated receipts, history entries, referral confirmations and reschedules do not add applications. Preserve distinct exact roles at one employer; normalize aliases only when supported. Explain multi-location/shared requisitions before combining lanes. Company counts are distinct from role counts and cannot simply be added across lanes.

Use mutually exclusive current stages whose rows sum to confirmed applications. Sent application email without employer receipt, preparation, failed delivery, contact enquiries and recruiter-led processes without verified formal submissions remain outside that denominator. Include those useful processes separately. Silence is not rejection, a user pass is not withdrawal, and a closed posting does not prove rejection. “No offer recorded” describes the available evidence.

Keep actual submission/event dates separate from report dates. Put unknown dates in their own bucket. State cohort sizes, observation window and outcome cutoff; recently pending applications are unfinished observations rather than failures. Do not compare sources or resume variants as causal effects when candidate, role or timing differences confound them.

## Interview rounds

Count documented completed employer recruiter/evaluation interviews and submitted assessments once each. One interview covering several topics is one round with several tags. Useful tags include recruiter, technical, design, hiring manager, behavioral and take-home, adapted to the profession.

Separate written prescreens from the headline live-interview/assessment count and state the convention. Exclude practice, tutorials, bookings, routine form questions and future rounds. Keep self-booked application-entry screens distinct from employer selection. Completion does not mean passing; an invitation does not mean completion. Preserve actual event dates, employer-confirmed next stages and unknown dates.

## Contacts and referrals

Count unique people actually contacted, not messages, drafted contacts or follow-ups. Preserve ambiguous aliases instead of merging guessed identities. Distinguish direct personal relationships, warm introductions, cold company outreach and agency/redirection routes. Social channel does not determine relationship type. Inbound recruiter contacts are not outbound cold outreach.

State whether personalized connection notes are included; keep bare connection requests separate. Count completed formal referrals separately from requests, offers to refer and introductions. User-confirmed completion counts with that provenance. A company-level referral does not establish a requisition, country or application for every role at that company. Report people referring, companies referred into and exact applications separately.

## Conversion analysis

Keep replies, helpful responses/offers, internal handoffs, completed referrals and interview progression as separate outcomes. Use unique people actually contacted as an outreach denominator. Use only people explicitly asked to refer for a referral-request denominator; unknown request intent stays unknown.

Record one credited acquisition outcome against its originating contact/introduction chain. A downstream recruiter reply can be a raw reply without being another independent acquisition success. A referral offer unused because another route exists is helpful but unused, not rejection. Inbound contract offers, employment offers and employee referrals are different outcomes.

Every rate needs a named cohort, numerator/denominator, cutoff and observation window when known. An incomplete subset cannot represent the candidate's whole network. Do not combine older and newer cohorts without noting their different response time. Referral-to-interview attribution requires role-level linkage; shared company names alone establish no causal connection. Missing dates, identities or mappings are limitations, not values to infer.

External benchmarks require current primary evidence with comparable population and stage definitions. Applications per vacancy are not applications per applicant, and accepting an existing offer is not the likelihood of receiving one. Describe a scenario as an assumption; never manufacture personalized hiring odds.
